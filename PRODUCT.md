## Product Purpose

Consent Guru manages website consent, policies, privacy intelligence, discovery, enforcement and evidence within organizations and sites. The migration adds runtime discovery, browser crawling, provenance-aware graphs, consent sessions and proofs, experiments and scoped secure transfers.

## Users

The dashboard serves organization members managing those workflows. Specific job titles are not established; privacy, compliance and technical operators are inferred from the existing capabilities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing backend behavior, authorization, organization/site isolation, consent semantics and all existing routes. Real backend results govern status and evidence; no invented metrics. Secure transfers receive plaintext on the server before encryption. No live tenant access or production migrations. The current UI revamp stays local and must not be pushed.

## Brand Commitments

The current implementation uses Manrope and Instrument Serif for public editorial reading, with a sage/ivory DPDP story and complete legal references. The evidence timeline, readable 2D Privacy Graph, and optional Three.js Digital Twin exploration remain part of this direction. Avoid decorative continuous motion and motion that steers consent choices.
