## 1. Thesis

A working privacy platform that makes evidence inspectable and guidance approachable. Operational pages support decisions; public reading surfaces reveal detail at the reader's pace.

## 2. Typography

Locally packaged Manrope for public reading and dashboard headings, Instrument Serif italic for the DPDP editorial accent, and Geist for existing operational body text/code. The new exhibit uses 52?86px overview type, 30?48px scene headings and short 18px captions. Fuller explanations use 16px/1.75 inside compact native modals. Compact layouts use 48px hero/34px scene headings. Local 11?13px control and metadata roles are intentional. Keep sentence case and readable prose.

## 4. Composition

One overview hero presents notice, choice, safeguards and evidence together. Seven scene models follow as sticky, scroll-stacking tiles on hydrated desktop layouts with motion enabled: purpose, notice, consent, withdrawal, children, responsibility and evidence. Purpose is a machined compass with 48 tick marks and five data tiles. Notice is an opening book with layered pages, leather covers, spine, bookmark and brass corners. Consent is a beveled console with two independent switches and recorded-choice indicators. Withdrawal uses separating keyed plugs, copper-colored pins and rubber cables. Children uses an arched guardian portal with two figurines, lock and gate. Responsibility is an inset vault with wheel, hinges and locking bolts. Evidence is a layered archive with backing, rivets, an Example seal and three labelled evidence nodes. The orb and its rings appear only in the Overview hero. Desktop panels alternate their visual/caption composition at 1100px and above. Each model stays inside its own panel. The seven mounted scene articles form a scroll-stacking tile gallery on hydrated desktop layouts with motion enabled. Zero-height natural-flow markers track the original chapter positions. Sticky tiles start at 164px plus 10px per chapter, with increasing z-index, 140px trailing spacing, a 1400px gallery perspective and top-centered transforms. Previous tiles shrink from 1 to 0.92 and tilt from 0 to -2 degrees as scrolling advances; their covered controls become inert. For viewport heights from 701px to 850px, chapter offsets narrow to 6px, panel padding becomes 20px and model stages become 280px tall. Compact layouts, heights at most 700px, reduced motion and SSR retain ordinary reading flow. A ResizeObserver also measures each tile against its sticky top and a 16px viewport reserve. If any tile cannot fit, including an expanded withdrawal history, the journey switches to ordinary reading flow even above the compact breakpoints. A layout effect preserves the active chapter at a 140px reading offset; viewport resizing retries the stack. A sticky horizontal progression row targets natural chapter markers to move between scenes. Short captions and ordinary consent-demo controls stay visible; object descriptions, chapter notes and the example evidence record open compact information windows. One native complete-guide disclosure retains all thirteen continuous chapters and their hash links. Operational surfaces retain shared forms, related tools and task tabs.

## 5. Motion and interaction

Motion supplies spring-smoothed tile scale and tilt from natural-flow chapter markers; Three.js supplies chapter-specific mechanisms, depth/light movement, pointer camera tilt and bounded state settling. Scroll moves the compass needle and data tiles, unfolds the notice book, turns the vault wheel and adjusts stage depth and lighting; pointer movement tilts the camera. The consent switches and withdrawal plugs settle from the existing bounded recorded-choice state. Draft choices do not activate optional paths. Frames stop after settling and pause when hidden. In desktop stacking mode, only the active and next chapter may mount a live stage within an 80px viewport margin. In normal reading flow, each stage uses the viewport gate alone, so mobile and short viewports render the real Three.js figures when visible, enabled and motion is allowed. Distant stages release their resources. Rendering density is capped at 1.25 below 768px and 1.5 on desktop, recalculated by ResizeObserver; canvas touch-action is pan-y. Covered tile controls are inert. Frames stop after settling and pause when hidden. Pointer raycasting on notice pages, guardian checkpoint and evidence plates has equivalent DOM controls. Responsibility has an inset vault with a locking wheel, hinges and bolts. Native dialog windows support Escape, close/backdrop dismissal, browser focus containment, body scroll lock and trigger focus restoration. Mobile and short viewports render the overview and seven Three.js chapter models in ordinary reading flow. Reduced motion, SSR/pre-hydration, no JavaScript and WebGL failure show a connected Notice/Choice/Safeguards/Evidence overview SVG plus the seven chapter diagrams and the same controls; interactive demo actions require hydration. No autoplay or consent steering.

DPDP choices stay in page memory: drafts do not grant, withdrawal closes current paths, history does not reactivate consent, guardian checkpoints do not verify identity, and the evidence example is not a signed receipt. No demonstration action calls an API, storage or SDK event. The optional Digital Twin retains configured relationships and its readable map alternative.
